### 🌀 [Complex Variables and Integral Transforms](./复变函数与积分变换)

> Wandering the complex plane, glimpsing cosmic symmetries through residues.  
> Includes lecture notes, classic examples, and poetic annotations on Fourier transforms.

### 🔭 [University Physics](./大学物理)

> From Newton's apple to Maxwell's fields,  
> force and light weave the fabric of the world.  
> Includes notes and mind maps on mechanics, electromagnetism, and thermodynamics.

### 💡 [Digital Circuits](./数字电路)

> A binary poem written in 0s and 1s,  
> where logic gates stitch the threads of reason.  
> Covers combinational logic, sequential circuit design, and a first look at Verilog.

### 🌊 [Analog Circuits](./模拟电路)

> Currents flow like streams and voltages rise like mountains.  
> Amplifiers carry the echoes of sound.  
> Includes transistor analysis, op-amp applications, and filter design materials.

### 🔬 [Electronic Circuit Laboratory I](./电子电路实验(上))

> Soldering wires, lighting up inspiration.  
> Lab reports, circuit diagrams, and debugging notes  
> record every spark of discovery.

### 🧮 [Discrete Mathematics](./离散数学)

> Sets, graphs, logic, and algebra  
> shape the skeleton of the abstract world.  
> Includes propositional logic, graph algorithms, and relational algebra resources.

### 📜 [Principles of Marxism](./马原)

> Rowing upstream through the river of dialectics,  
> seeing how history is written through practice.  
> Organizes core concepts, classic excerpts, and reflective study notes.

### 📡 [Signal Analysis](./信号分析)

> Waves whisper and spectra hum;  
> information dances between time and frequency.  
> From Fourier to Laplace, every transform reveals a hidden melody,  
> while key notes and mind maps capture the rhythm of signals.

### 🤖 [Introduction to Artificial Intelligence](./人工智能导论)

> Intelligence like light, algorithms like wind;  
> at the meeting point of data and rules, the future quietly awakens.  
> From perception to reasoning, from deep learning to reinforcement learning,  
> core concepts and mind maps chart the galaxy of intelligence.

### 💾 [Data Structures](./数据结构)

> Links form veins, nodes form forests;  
> data quietly grows in the world of logic.  
> Stacks and queues, trees and graphs: every path is a line of order,  
> and study materials trace the texture of information.

### 📊 [Operations Research](./运筹学)

> Equations as beacons, optimization as sails;  
> decisions navigate the ocean of mathematics.  
> From linear programming to dynamic programming, every model shapes a piece of wisdom.

### 🧭 [Xi Jinping Thought Review Materials](./习概复习资料)

> Outlines, question banks, and page indexes  
> help turn thick materials into a review path.  
> Useful for quickly locating key points before exams.

## 🌱 How to Use

- All materials are shared for learning and non-commercial use only
- Start from the course folder, then combine lecture notes, past papers, and review outlines
- Contributions, corrections, and formatting improvements are welcome through Issues or Pull Requests
- If this repository helps you, a Star is already a kind bit of support
